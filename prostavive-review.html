Key Ingredients & How They Work

🌿

Saw Palmetto

The most studied botanical for prostate support. Saw palmetto's fatty acid and sterol content inhibits 5-alpha reductase — the enzyme that converts testosterone to DHT, the primary driver of prostate tissue enlargement. Multiple systematic reviews support its efficacy for reducing lower urinary tract symptoms associated with BPH.

🔬

Beta-Sitosterol

A plant sterol found in many foods but rarely in therapeutic concentrations. Beta-sitosterol reduces prostate inflammation through prostaglandin pathway inhibition and has demonstrated improvements in urinary flow rates and post-void residual volume in randomized controlled trials — making it one of the most evidence-supported prostate ingredients available.

🔩

Zinc

The prostate gland contains higher concentrations of zinc than virtually any other tissue in the male body. Adequate zinc is essential for prostate cell apoptosis (programmed cell death) and immune defense within the gland. Zinc deficiency is disproportionately associated with prostate dysfunction and inflammatory changes.

🌱

Tongkat Ali (Longjack)

A Southeast Asian root with clinically documented testosterone-supporting properties. Tongkat ali reduces sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG), which effectively increases free testosterone availability. It also supports stress resilience and libido — addressing the vitality dimension of male hormonal wellness beyond just the prostate.

🧠

Ashwagandha

Chronic cortisol elevation suppresses testosterone production and worsens systemic inflammation — both relevant to prostate health. Ashwagandha's adaptogenic action on the HPA axis helps restore hormonal balance, with multiple trials showing meaningful reductions in cortisol and improvements in testosterone markers in stressed men.

⚗️

Boron & Ginseng

Boron supports detoxification of estradiol and DHT metabolites through its effects on steroid hormone metabolism — important for men whose hormonal profile skews toward estrogen dominance. Ginseng (Panax) adds adaptogenic vitality support, improved circulation, and anti-inflammatory activity that complements the formula's prostate-specific ingredients.

Are There Any Side Effects?

ProstaVive is well-tolerated by the overwhelming majority of men who use it. The most frequently reported early side effect is mild gastrointestinal sensitivity during the first 1–2 weeks — this typically resolves as the body adjusts to the botanical compound load in the formula, particularly saw palmetto which can occasionally cause mild digestive discomfort on an empty stomach.

Men taking blood thinners or anticoagulants should discuss saw palmetto and ginseng use with their physician, as both compounds have mild platelet-aggregation effects. Similarly, tongkat ali's effect on hormone levels means men with hormone-sensitive conditions should consult a doctor before use.

Prostate cancer screening remains important regardless of supplement use. ProstaVive is a wellness support tool, not a diagnostic or treatment product. US men over 50 — or over 40 with a family history — should maintain regular PSA testing and urological check-ups independent of supplementation.
