Key Ingredients & How They Work

CelluCare's formula draws from over a dozen plant-based compounds. Here are the most pharmacologically relevant:

🌿

Gymnema Sylvestre

Contains gymnemic acids that temporarily block sweet taste receptors on the tongue and slow glucose absorption in the intestines. This dual action helps curb sugar cravings while blunting post-meal glucose spikes — one of the most clinically explored botanicals for blood sugar support.

🍃

Banaba Leaf Extract

Rich in corosolic acid, which mimics insulin's action by facilitating glucose uptake into cells. Sometimes called "natural insulin," banaba has been studied in several trials for its ability to reduce fasting blood glucose levels in healthy adults.

🟡

Turmeric Rhizome (Curcumin)

Curcumin is one of nature's most studied anti-inflammatories. Chronic low-grade inflammation is now recognized as a core driver of insulin resistance. By addressing this upstream cause, turmeric contributes to the formula's long-term metabolic benefits rather than just short-term glucose control.

🍫

Cocoa Bean Extract

Provides flavanols that support endothelial function and improve peripheral blood flow — important for efficient glucose delivery to muscle tissue. Also provides antioxidant protection against oxidative stress linked to glycation damage.

🌱

Eleuthero Root

An adaptogenic herb that helps the body respond more efficiently to physiological stress — including the metabolic stress of blood sugar fluctuations. Eleuthero has been associated with improved stamina and reduced fatigue in multiple human studies.

🫐

Juniper Berry & Pine Bark Extract

Both deliver concentrated polyphenol antioxidants that support vascular health and reduce oxidative markers associated with metabolic dysfunction. Pine bark extract (as OPC complex) has demonstrated blood pressure and microcirculation benefits in clinical research.
